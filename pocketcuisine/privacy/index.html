Pocket Cuisine

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

In short: Pocket Cuisine does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, does not require an account, and Typin does not collect gameplay or personal data through the game.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pocket Cuisine, developed and published by Typin, handles information when you use the game on Android.

2. Data collected by Typin

Pocket Cuisine does not require you to create an account and does not send gameplay analytics, advertising identifiers, crash analytics, location data, contacts, or other personal information to Typin.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Typin does not operate a server that receives or stores this local game data.

3. Google Play and in-app purchases

Pocket Cuisine is distributed through Google Play and includes optional in-app purchases. Google may process information needed to distribute the game, handle payments, prevent fraud, and manage purchases under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Typin may be able to view limited purchase information provided through Google Play when needed to verify a purchase, provide support, or process a refund. This information is not used for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

4. Third-party analytics and advertising

Pocket Cuisine does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs operated by Typin. The game does not display third-party advertising.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because Typin does not maintain user accounts or collect gameplay or personal data through Pocket Cuisine, there is no Typin-hosted user profile or analytics history to delete.

Locally stored game data can be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the game. Information held by Google Play, including purchase records, is managed by Google under its own policies and legal obligations.

6. Children's privacy

Typin does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Pocket Cuisine. Because the game does not use Typin-operated analytics, advertising, or user accounts, Typin does not receive age or profile information about players through the game.

7. External services and links

The game or its store listing may link to services operated by third parties. Typin is not responsible for the privacy practices of those services, and their own policies apply when you use them.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Pocket Cuisine's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Pocket Cuisine's data practices, contact contact@typin.games.

This policy describes Typin's current data practices for Pocket Cuisine. Services provided directly by Google, including Google Play distribution and billing, are governed by Google's own policies.
